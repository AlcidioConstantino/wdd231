MozHub

Website Site Plan

1. Site Name

MozHub

The name MozHub combines "Moz", referring to Mozambique, with "Hub", meaning a central place where people and services can connect.

The name was selected because the website is designed to be a central platform where people in Mozambique can discover professional services and where service providers can promote their skills and businesses.

Possible domain: mozhub.co.mz

2. Site Purpose

MozHub is a platform designed to connect customers with professional service providers in Mozambique. The website will help users find professionals based on their service, location, and availability.

The site will provide information about professionals and businesses, including their services, profiles, locations, contact information, and other relevant details that can help customers choose a service provider.

Initial service categories may include electricians, plumbers, mechanics, tutors, photographers, cleaners, construction workers, beauty professionals, and technology professionals.

3. Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions that potential visitors to the MozHub website may have:

  • Where can I find a reliable electrician near my location?
  • How can I find a plumber to repair a problem at my house?
  • How can I contact a professional who offers the service I need?
  • How can I create a profile and advertise my professional services on MozHub?

4. Color Scheme

The MozHub color scheme is designed to create a professional, trustworthy, and modern appearance while keeping the interface simple and easy to use.

Navy

Hex: #123554

Used for the header, main headings, navigation, and important interface elements.

Dark Blue

Hex: #0B2743

Used for section borders and other elements that need stronger contrast.

Yellow

Hex: #F4D35E

Used for accents, highlights, dividers, and call-to-action elements.

White

Hex: #FFFFFF

Used for section backgrounds and text displayed on dark backgrounds.

5. Typography

MozHub will use two primary fonts. The typography will provide a clean and modern appearance while maintaining good readability.

Poppins

Use: Main headings, section headings, navigation, buttons, and important titles.

Example: Find a Professional Near You

Open Sans

Use: Body text, descriptions, forms, paragraphs, and general information.

Example: Find professional services available in your area.
